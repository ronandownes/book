The Deep Blue photograph shows museum hardware. The Lee Sedol photograph was taken after the match. Captions distinguish these from match photographs.

The SVGs are original drawings for Irish School Maths. Game diagrams use factual moves from the unannotated records below and do not copy commentary or film stills:

- https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/AlphaGo/LeeSedol/2.sgf
- https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/AlphaGo/LeeSedol/4.sgf
- Record index: https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/AlphaGo/

`scripts/generate_ty_news_diagrams.py` recreates the static SVGs, including the actual positions after Black 37 at P10 and White 78 at L11. It checks those coordinates and handles captures. The Go capture teaching illustration is explicitly separate from the match records.

Videos remain with their publishers. Links and the official film's normal embedded player are used; video files are not copied. Public viewing does not give the films the photographs' Creative Commons licences.
